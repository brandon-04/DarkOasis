Dark Oasis

Dark Oasis is a brand new 2d, platformer game being developed and will soon release. The game follows Owain Glyndwr, a welsh hero who fell into an underwater cavern during a battle with the saxons. Before he drowns he is given the ability to breathe underwater by a magical siren who saves him. He then use some rope and a spear to make a hook which will help him espace the treacharous cave.
